Hills I will die on

  1. 01

    The East Coast needs to reimagine public transit

    Boston should rebuild its transit as an elevated, rubber-tired metro. In a compact city with stops close together, rubber tires grip harder than steel wheels on steel rails, so trains accelerate and brake faster and the whole route speeds up; up on a viaduct and off steel rails, they run quieter too. And the country needs high-speed rail around its perimeter, starting with the East Coast.
