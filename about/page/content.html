Biography

Where research becomes software

Alex Crystal is a generative AI engineer at the MITRE Corporation, in Generative and Agentic AI Engineering. Recent work there: a hierarchical topic model, and topic nodes added to an agentic knowledge graph for Kohai R6.

The path ran through a BS in Data Science at the University of Rochester (completed 2022) and an MS in Artificial Intelligence at Northeastern University (completed 2024). A graduate internship at MITRE spanned 2020 to 2024, in Human Language Technology: misinformation detection, disinformation tactics, aviation forecasting, a science-and-technology knowledge graph and authorship attribution.

Research has run in parallel: biological-network analysis at the University of Rochester Medical Center, including contributions to the mBONITA package; deep-learning research in time-series forecasting through the TRIPODS REU; and, as a co-author, Executable Network Models of Integrated Multiomics Data in the Journal of Proteome Research (2023).

Outside work: Nordic ski racing and running, hiking in New Hampshire, chess, and mountain photography. Some of it has become software too, such as Birkie Race Intelligence, an explorer for American Birkebeiner results.

Areas of focus

Three threads through the work

  1. 01

    Language systems

    Transformer models that detect, attribute and classify text.

    • TextCraftMITRE, 2023

      A Siamese network for authorship attribution, 97% across 119 labels, and a reward signal for RL-fine-tuned language models.

    • Bot DetectorMITRE, 2020

      A RoBERTa discriminator and GPT-2 generator for social-media misinformation detection.

    • Summer Hackathon2023

      First place after augmenting training data and fine-tuning a transformer for phishing-site classification.

  2. 02

    Knowledge graphs and topic models

    Topic models and knowledge graphs that organize large collections of documents.

    • Kohai R6MITRE, 2025–

      A hierarchical topic model, and topic nodes added to an agentic knowledge graph.

    • S&TI 2.0MITRE, 2022

      Contributions to a science-and-technology knowledge graph, and deep topic models built with TMNT to cluster academic papers.

  3. 03

    Forecasting and scientific modeling

    Forecasting and network models built from operational and scientific data.

    • ASIASMITRE, 2022

      Multivariate aviation-forecasting models fine-tuned with Bayesian Hyperband optimization.

      Deep-learning research in time-series forecasting.
