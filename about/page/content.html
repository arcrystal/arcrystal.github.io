Now

Where research becomes software

I’m a generative AI engineer at MITRE, in Generative and Agentic AI Engineering. Lately that means a hierarchical topic model, and topic nodes in an agentic knowledge graph for Kohai R6.

Before that I spent four years as a graduate AI intern in MITRE’s Human Language Technology group, working on misinformation detection, aviation forecasting, a science-and-technology knowledge graph and authorship attribution, while finishing a BS in Data Science at Rochester and an MS in Artificial Intelligence at Northeastern.

Things I’ve built

Three that I’m proud of

  1. Personal project · 2021 to Aug 2026

    AI Power Map

    A sourced history of the models, agents, chips, companies and rules that moved the AI frontier, and who gained or lost leverage each time. Every one of its 224 moments answers the same six questions, with 312 citations behind them. I wrote the research, the script that turns it into data, and the explorer.

    Open the map ↗Download the sourced Markdown ↓

  2. App · Data

    Birkie Race Intelligence

    Every finisher of the American Birkebeiner’s Skate and Classic races, explorable: starting waves, racer history, how a result compares, and where a racer is likely to place. I built it after racing the Birkie myself.

    Open the app ↗My races →

  3. Published research · 2023

    mBONITA

    Executable network models that combine layers of omics data to find influential genes and run pathway analysis on a network’s topology. I worked on biological-network analysis methods and the mBONITA package at the University of Rochester Medical Center, and I’m a co-author on the paper in the Journal of Proteome Research.

    Read the paper ↗Code on GitHub ↗
