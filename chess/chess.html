01 · Playing style

Playing style

Three openings anchor the repertoire: the Réti, the Scotch Game and the O’Kelly Sicilian. The Scotch is defined by its third move, 3. d4, which opens the centre at once.

Featured game

Until a game loads from Chess.com, this is a demonstration of the Scotch Game: a standard opening line, not a game Alex played.
